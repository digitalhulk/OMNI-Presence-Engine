# Governance Principles

1. Root cause before patch.
2. Evidence before assertion.
3. User value before vanity metrics.
4. Human usability and machine readability must coexist.
5. Preserve approved experience during technical optimization.
6. Treat platform-specific tactics as adapters, not the core architecture.
7. Prefer standards and first-party documentation.
8. Record uncertainty explicitly.
9. Version important changes.
10. Every recommendation needs validation criteria.
11. Security, privacy and accessibility are first-class requirements.
12. Do not optimize one channel by silently damaging another.
