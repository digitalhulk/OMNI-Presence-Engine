# Evidence Policy

Evidence priority:
1. Official platform documentation and standards.
2. Direct measurements and controlled experiments.
3. Reproducible technical testing.
4. High-quality independent research.
5. Expert consensus.
6. Community reports.
7. Anecdotes.

Every material tactic must be tagged FACT, HYPOTHESIS, EXPERIMENT or DEPRECATED.

Claims about ranking, AI retrieval, citations or platform behavior must identify the platform and date because behavior can change.

Do not turn correlation into causation. Do not treat a metric as proof of business impact without validation.
