# Module Specification — 20 Modules

Each module owns a distinct problem and exposes inputs, checks, outputs and validation.

01 ENTITY — identity, ownership, identifiers, relationships, consistency.
02 INFRASTRUCTURE — domain, DNS, hosting, CDN, server, data, APIs, caching, observability.
03 CODE — HTML semantics, head, metadata, CSS, JS, JSON/JSON-LD, forms, runtime and third-party code.
04 CRAWL — discovery, robots, sitemaps, bot access, rendering access and crawl efficiency.
05 INDEX — status, canonicalization, duplication, indexability, freshness and rendering.
06 SEMANTICS — entities, topics, queries, relationships, taxonomy and knowledge structures.
07 CONTENT — usefulness, intent, topical coverage, evidence, freshness, authorship, FAQs, reviews and UGC.
08 MEDIA — image/video/audio/graphics quality, metadata, accessibility, delivery and discovery.
09 SEARCH — web, local, image, video, news and shopping discovery.
10 AI SEARCH — retrieval, grounding, answer inclusion, citations, recommendations and platform adapters.
11 AUTHORITY — links, mentions, PR, references, expertise, reviews and reputation.
12 LOCAL — business profiles, maps, NAP, service areas, hours, reviews and local pages.
13 UX — architecture, navigation, hierarchy, interaction, mobile and task completion.
14 ACCESSIBILITY — semantic structure, keyboard, focus, alternatives, contrast, forms, captions and assistive access.
15 PERFORMANCE — network, loading, rendering, runtime, frames, memory, energy and experience preservation.
16 SECURITY — transport, headers, authentication, input safety, dependencies, secrets, WAF and abuse controls.
17 LANGUAGE — locale, translation, transcreation, scripts, hreflang, Unicode, fonts and cultural/search intent.
18 ANALYTICS — search, AI visibility, behavior, CRM, ads, server/CDN data, attribution and revenue.
19 CONVERSION — lead, qualification, booking, checkout, sales, retention and referral.
20 CONTINUOUS OPTIMIZATION — observation, diagnosis, prioritization, testing, deployment, regression and learning.

Cross-cutting requirements: evidence, ownership, dependencies, failure modes, privacy, accessibility, security, measurement and validation.
