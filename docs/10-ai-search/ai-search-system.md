# AI Search System

AI visibility is modeled as retrieval and recommendation readiness across answer systems.

## Inputs
Entity clarity, factual content, structured data, accessible page content, source reputation, topical coverage, freshness, links/mentions and machine-readable context.

## Outcomes
Retrieval -> grounding -> answer inclusion -> citation -> recommendation -> action.

## Platform adapters
Google AI experiences, Bing/Copilot, ChatGPT search, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and other systems should be tracked independently because retrieval and citation behavior differs.

## Instrumentation
Track cited pages, cited queries where available, source visibility, referral traffic, branded demand, conversions and qualitative answer accuracy. Do not treat citation activity as proof of ranking or authority.

## Governance
AEO/GEO/LLMO are lenses. Do not create duplicate foundations or rely on undocumented hacks. Prefer official platform guidance and controlled experiments.
