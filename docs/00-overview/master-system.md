# Master System

## Purpose
OPE is a unified model for online presence from infrastructure to revenue.

## Lifecycle
ZERO -> EXIST -> DISCOVER -> UNDERSTAND -> TRUST -> RANK -> ANSWER -> CITE -> RECOMMEND -> ENGAGE -> CONVERT -> RETAIN -> AUTHORITY.

## Module map
01 Entity: identity and ownership.
02 Infrastructure: domain, DNS, hosting, CDN, server, data and APIs.
03 Code: HTML, CSS, JS, metadata, structured data, forms and runtime.
04 Crawl: robots, sitemaps, discovery, bot access and rendering access.
05 Index: canonicalization, status, duplication, indexability and freshness.
06 Semantics: entities, topics, queries, relationships and taxonomy.
07 Content: useful text, documents, data, FAQs, reviews and UGC.
08 Media: images, video, audio, graphics and interactive assets.
09 Search: traditional, local, image, video, news and shopping discovery.
10 AI Search: retrieval, answers, citations and AI visibility across systems.
11 Authority: links, mentions, PR, reviews, references and reputation.
12 Local: maps, profiles, NAP, service areas, reviews and local landing pages.
13 UX: information architecture, navigation, interaction and conversion journeys.
14 Accessibility: semantic structure, keyboard, focus, contrast, alternatives and assistive technology.
15 Performance: network, loading, rendering, runtime, frames, memory and energy.
16 Security: transport, headers, auth, input safety, dependencies and abuse controls.
17 Language: locale, translation, transcreation, hreflang, scripts and cultural intent.
18 Analytics: search, AI visibility, behavior, CRM, ads, attribution and revenue.
19 Conversion: leads, qualification, checkout, sales, retention and referrals.
20 Continuous Optimization: observe, diagnose, fix, test and learn.

## Universal audit questions
What? Why? How? Where? For whom? When? Dependency? Failure mode? Evidence? Measurement? Validation?
