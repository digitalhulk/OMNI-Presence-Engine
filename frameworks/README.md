# Framework Lenses

These are views over the OPE core.

## SEO
Discovery, crawling, indexing, relevance, search presentation and technical foundations.

## AEO
Answer-oriented information architecture and concise, verifiable answers for answer surfaces.

## GEO
Optimization for generative retrieval, grounding, source selection and citation visibility.

## LLMO
Improving machine-readable context and source usefulness for large-language-model workflows.

## SXO
Search-to-experience continuity: intent, relevance, usability and task completion.

## CRO
Turning qualified attention into measurable business outcomes.

## AI Agents
Making legitimate public workflows understandable and operable by automated systems through clear structure, accessible controls, robust forms, predictable navigation and explicit states.

Governance: these lenses may share controls. Do not duplicate work merely because the acronym changes.
