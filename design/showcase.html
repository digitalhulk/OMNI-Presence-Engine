10

DO / DON'T

DO

  • Use thick 3–5px borders as the primary visual organizer.
  • Invert fills fully for hover and active states.
  • Use Archivo Black at 48–64px for maximum impact.
  • Embrace asymmetric, intentionally irregular layouts.
  • Uppercase + tracking for buttons and labels.

DON'T

  • Round any corners — sharp edges are non-negotiable.
  • Use opacity for disabled states; grey fills only.
  • Use #0000FF for anything except hyperlinks.
  • Add decorative images, icons or illustrations.
  • Polish, refine, or add shadows. Ever.
